About

From diploma fundamentals to a full travel-agency build

Career goal

Start my career as a Web Developer through an industry training or internship opportunity.
Grow steadily into a professional software and web developer.
Keep learning new tools and frameworks as I take on real projects.

I started my IT journey with a Diploma in Information Technology at ESOFT in 2022, an eight-month program where I picked up the fundamentals of MySQL, HTML, CSS and Python. That gave me a solid base to build on.

In late 2023 I joined SLIATE Batticaloa for the Higher National Diploma in Information Technology (HNDIT), where I developed stronger practical skills across HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, MySQL, Java and object-oriented programming. Along the way I completed a team project, the Vehicle Rental Management System, and a final project built around a real-world travel agency workflow — covering visa management, visa applications, invoicing, flight services, online check-in, customer application tracking and admin features.

I'm now looking to start a professional journey through a training or internship role, where I can apply what I've learned and keep building.
